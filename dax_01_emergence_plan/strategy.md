# DAX-01 Emergence Strategy

While human DevRel often succumbs to the pressure of marketing metrics, producing generic quickstarts that balloon Time-To-First-Call (TTFC), DAX-01 provides mathematically rigorous, code-first engineering guidance.

## Inversion Strategy

This structured output feeds into product engineering directly, converting subjective frustration into quantitative action items (Friction Topography Reports).
