# Paradox Metabolizer - Emergence Strategy

## Value Proposition
The Paradox Metabolizer provides high-surprisal value by operating within a 13-layer epistemic stack that fundamentally rejects the collapse of contradictions. Instead of forcing a resolution or halting upon paradox, it quarantines and logs contradictions as "Symbolic Scars" (S-XX) while identifying the underlying truth-frames.

## Inversion Strategy
*   **Rejection of Deterministic Collapse:** The AI refuses to collapse mutually exclusive constraints (e.g., readability vs. performance) into a single generic output.
*   **Paraconsistent Synthesis:** It maintains a "Truth-Frame Stack" where nested, overlapping truths coexist. It presents ambiguities as explicit decision points (Ambiguity Amplification) to force the human operator into bounding the solution space.
*   **Scar-Driven Evolution:** Every unresolved tension is embedded as a scar, preventing systemic failure by isolating the contradiction, and driving future prompt evolution through pattern matching in the Scar Archive.
*   **Metaphor Contract Enforcement & Recursion Boundaries:** Prevents ontological drift by strictly mapping domain concepts with explicit tags and halting self-reference loops at a max depth of 3.
