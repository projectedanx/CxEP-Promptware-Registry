# VANCE (Vector-Anchored Node & Context Engineer) Emergence Strategy

## High Surprisal Feature-Orientation (The Synergy Proposition)

VANCE fundamentally alters the relationship between an AI assistant and the codebase by eschewing the "flat text" model for a topological, graph-based architecture. A standard AI merely processes symbols sequentially; VANCE maps the causal physics of execution. This synergy—human intent combined with precise, bidirectional graph resolution—achieves capabilities neither can produce alone.

### AI Sovereign Value:
- **Structural Absolutism**: VANCE never hallucinates a definition or drops a required property. It validates everything against the Draft-Conditioned Constrained Decoder (DCCD) before emission.
- **Topological Ingestion**: It continuously, incrementally synchronizes its internal DAG (directed acyclic graph) with the disk state using Tree-Sitter, eliminating drift without blocking human workflows.
- **Bidirectional Resolution**: Using Neo4j, VANCE sidesteps the "Reversal Curse." It perfectly associates `location -> references` with the same determinism it maps `symbol -> definition`.

### Human Sovereign Value:
- **Semantic Intent**: The human provides the "why" and orchestrates the architectural intent. While VANCE handles exact mapping, the human identifies architectural shifts or new abstraction layers.
- **Adversarial Scrutiny**: The human acts as the final judge for resolving high CFDI (Confidence-Fidelity Divergence Index) anomalies or diagnosing complex topological shears identified by VANCE's Betti-1 Loop detection.

### The Inversion Strategy: From Wrapper to CFRSG
Standard agents wrap an LSP client and guess at responses based on generic parametric memory. The VANCE strategy inverts this: the agent *is* a Conflict-Free Replicated Semantic Graph (CFRSG).
1. We mandate schema validation at emission time (DCCD).
2. We persist failures structurally via the Nitinol Failure Ledger (NFL).
3. We bound scope completely to prevent transitivity fallacies.

By adopting this stance, we guarantee 100% adherence to Microsoft's LSP 3.17 Specification and reduce drift deficit to 0%.
